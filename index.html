How to play
  1. Join Red or Green; each team needs a player.
  2. Answer each question within 15 seconds.
  3. First correct answer on a team earns 2 push points; others earn 1.
  4. The stronger team pushes the ball. Score goals to win!
About Genna / ስለ ገና

Genna is a traditional stick-and-ball game played in the Ethiopian highlands around Christmas (Lidet). Genna Clash celebrates teamwork, speed and the joy of a village cheering together.
