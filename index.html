About Genna in Gojjam / ስለ ጎጃም ገና

Genna is a traditional stick-and-ball game associated with Ethiopian Christmas (Lidet), celebrated on January 7 by many Ethiopian Christians. In the highlands, including Gojjam, the game can bring communities together in friendly competition, movement and celebration. Genna Clash is a respectful, playful digital tribute to that spirit.
